Privacy

What We Collect
and Why

A plain-language summary of the information EagleVision handles, for teachers, schools, and families.

The Short Version

  • Students don't create accounts. They join with a session code and a name they type in.
  • We don't use ads, analytics, or tracking cookies.
  • We don't record the live video.
  • Session details are kept on our server only while the session is running.

Joining a Session

To join, a student enters a session code and a name (up to 15 characters). The name is shown to the instructor and others in the session so they know who is connected. A first name or nickname works fine.

The code and name are sent to our server, which keeps them in memory while the session is running and removes them when everyone has left. Our server's logs may also briefly record names and session codes while we fix problems.

Your browser also keeps the code and name for the current tab, so a page refresh doesn't kick you out. They are cleared when the tab is closed.

Live Video, Annotations, and Snapshots

  • Video. Only the instructor's camera is streamed. Students' cameras and microphones are never used. The stream passes through our streaming server to the people in the session and is not recorded.
  • Annotations. Drawings and text added during a session are sent through our server to others in the same session. They are not saved after the session ends.
  • Snapshots. Snapshots stay in the browser that took them unless the instructor chooses to share one with the session. Shared snapshots are sent through our server and are not stored. Anything you download is saved only on your own device.

Eagle AI (Beta)

Eagle AI is optional and needs an access code. When someone uses it, the current microscope image, and any question they type, is sent to our server and then to Google's Gemini service to generate an answer. Google handles that request under its own terms. Our server's logs may record the start of a typed question.

Past Eagle AI answers are saved in that browser only, so you can look back at them. Clearing the browser's site data removes them.

Settings Saved in Your Browser

EagleVision remembers a few preferences on your device: your color theme, whether colorblind symbols are on, and the size of the side panel. These never leave your browser.

The Contact Form

If you fill out the contact form, we receive your name, country, institution, role, email, your answers about equipment, and your message. The form is delivered to us by Formspree. We use it only to reply to you and help set up EagleVision.

Other Services Your Browser Loads

Like most websites, EagleVision loads some files from other services. Each one sees your device's IP address when your browser connects to it:

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts provides the icons.
  • Google Drive hosts the demo video on the homepage.
  • jsDelivr provides the video streaming library.
  • OpenCV (docs.opencv.org) provides image tools in the session workspace.
