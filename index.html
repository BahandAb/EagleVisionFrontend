Who It's For

Built for Every Learning Environment

school

K–12 Classrooms

One microscope, every student's screen. Teachers stream live slides while students annotate, count cells, and ask questions — no individual eyepieces needed. Brings microscopy to schools that can't afford a full lab set.

science

College & University Labs

Run interactive lab sessions for large lecture groups or hybrid cohorts. Instructors guide the view while students collaborate on annotations in real time — from the front row or remotely.

local_hospital

Medical & Clinical Education

Teaching pathology, histology, or clinical microscopy? Stream live slides to residents and students simultaneously, with AI-assisted identification to support learning and discussion.

Real-World Testing
